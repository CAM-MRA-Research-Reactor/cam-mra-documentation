The MAPLE reactor was discussed as a potential reference for the reactor design. MAPLE was designed for isotope production at approximately 10 MWth and, although no longer operational, remains at Chalk River. The design used a reactor pool, forced-convection cooling, light-water coolant.

The team discussed investigating a similar overall design approach. Key decisions will include fuel type and enrichment, moderator, coolant, and fuel geometry. An enrichment of approximately 5% was suggested as a potential design target, and standardized fuel bundle designs, including configurations similar to CANDU fuel, will be investigated.

The CNSC Vendor Design Review process and REGDOC-3.5.4 were discussed as potential guidance for developing and reviewing the reactor concept. Initial design work should focus on scoping calculations for different core configurations to estimate core size and geometry. The surrounding systems and facility can then be developed around the selected core configuration.
